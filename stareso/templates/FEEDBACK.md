# Test feedback

go under `## During build M[n] yyyy-mm-dd`. Each point is one line in the user's words, tagged [bug] / [change] /
[idea] / [decision to confirm], and says what became of it once segmented (→ slice M20.3, → IDEAS, → won't fix: reason).
Only the newest two blocks stay here; older ones move to FEEDBACK-DONE.md at the end of a test.
